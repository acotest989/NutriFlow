Delete your NutriFlow account & data

App: NutriFlow · Developer contact: chillibrimedia@gmail.com

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to chillibrimedia@gmail.com from the email address associated with your NutriFlow account.
  2. Use the subject line "NutriFlow account deletion request".
  3. We will verify the request and permanently delete your account and data.

What gets deleted

Upon a verified request we permanently delete your account and all associated data, including your logged meals, exercises, daily goals, and hydration records. This action is irreversible.

What is kept, and for how long

We do not retain your personal account data after deletion. Account and health/fitness data are removed within 30 days of a verified request. Minimal, non-identifying server logs (kept for security and abuse prevention) may persist for a short period before being rotated out, and any records we are legally required to keep are retained only as long as the law requires.

See also our Privacy Policy.
