NutriFlow — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 June 2026

NutriFlow ("the app", "we") is a personal diet, hydration, and exercise tracker. This policy explains what data we collect, how it is used, and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Account data: your email address and an authentication credential, used to create and secure your account.
  • Health & fitness data you enter: logged meals, exercises, daily calorie/macronutrient goals, and hydration amounts.
  • Technical data: standard server logs (e.g. timestamps, error logs) needed to operate and secure the service.

How we use it

  • To provide the core features — storing and displaying your logs, goals, and trends.
  • To generate AI features (nutrition estimates, recipes, and coaching feedback). The text you submit for these features is sent to Google's Gemini API for processing and is not used to train models by us.

Where your data is stored

Account and health/fitness data are stored in Supabase (PostgreSQL), protected by row-level security so each account can access only its own data. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Third-party services

  • Supabase — authentication and database.
  • Google Gemini API — AI processing of content you submit to AI features.
  • Google Cloud Run — application hosting.

Data retention & deletion

Your data is retained while your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing the address below; we will remove your account and data within 30 days.

Children

NutriFlow is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the date above.
