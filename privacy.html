NutriFlow — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

NutriFlow ("the app", "we") is a personal diet, hydration, and exercise tracker, available on the web and as an Android app on Google Play. This policy explains what data we collect, how it is used, and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Account data: your email address and an authentication credential, used to create and secure your account. If you choose Continue with Google, we receive your Google account's email address.
  • Profile data you enter: your goal, sex, age, height, weight, target weight, activity level, dietary preference, food restrictions/allergies, preferred workouts, units, and app language. These personalize your calorie/macro goal and the AI features.
  • Health & fitness data you enter: logged meals, exercises, daily calorie/macronutrient goals, and hydration amounts.
  • Meal photos you choose to analyze: when you use Snap a Meal, the photo you take or pick is sent for AI analysis. We do not store the photo.
  • Technical data: standard server logs (e.g. timestamps, error logs) needed to operate and secure the service. The Android app also uses Google Play Integrity (via Firebase App Check) to confirm that requests come from the genuine app on a genuine device.

How we use it

  • To provide the core features — storing and displaying your logs, goals, and trends.
  • To generate AI features (nutrition estimates, meal-photo analysis, barcode estimates, recipes, and coaching feedback). The text or photo you submit, together with the relevant profile preferences and that day's logs for coaching, is sent to Google's Gemini API for processing — through our server on the web, and through Google Firebase AI Logic in the Android app. It is not used to train models by us.
  • To look up food and exercise information: barcodes you scan or type are looked up in Open Food Facts, food search terms in USDA FoodData Central, and the exercise catalog is downloaded from wger. Only the barcode or search term is sent — no account data.

Android app permissions

  • Camera — used only while you scan a barcode or take a meal photo. Barcodes are read on the device; a meal photo is sent only when you choose to analyze it.
  • Internet / network state — to sync your data and show when you're offline.

Where your data is stored

Account and health/fitness data are stored in Supabase (PostgreSQL), protected by row-level security so each account can access only its own data. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). The Android app keeps a copy of your last-synced data on your device so it can be viewed offline; it is removed when you sign out. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Third-party services

  • Supabase — authentication and database.
  • Google Gemini API (via Google Firebase AI Logic in the Android app) — AI processing of content you submit to AI features.
  • Google Firebase App Check / Play Integrity — protects the AI features from abuse (Android app).
  • Google Sign-In — optional sign-in with your Google account.
  • Open Food Facts, USDA FoodData Central, wger — public food and exercise databases.
  • Google Cloud Run — web application hosting.

Data retention & deletion

Your data is retained while your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the app (Delete Account in the footer), or by emailing the address below, in which case we will remove your account and data within 30 days. Reset Data in the app deletes all logged meals, exercises, and hydration while keeping your account.

Children

NutriFlow is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
